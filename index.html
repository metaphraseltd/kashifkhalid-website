Written Language Services

Translation,
Transcription & More

Translation Transcription Proofreading DTP

Accurate written communication demands the same precision as spoken interpretation — and often greater care. I provide professional written translation between English, Hindi, Urdu and Punjabi for legal documents, official correspondence, business materials, medical records and personal certificates. Every translation is faithful to the original in meaning, tone and intent.

Beyond translation, I offer audio and video transcription, converting recordings into accurate written text in English or the source language — invaluable for legal proceedings, research, media and compliance purposes. Editing, proofreading and quality-checking services are also available to ensure existing translations meet professional standards before use.

For publishers, agencies and organisations working with Hindi, Urdu or Punjabi scripts, I also provide desktop publishing (DTP) support — ensuring translated documents are correctly formatted, right-to-left text is properly typeset, and the final output is print- or publication-ready to the highest professional standard.
